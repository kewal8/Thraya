# /v2 surface brief

Scope: the Thraya Homeopathy landing page, Copy 2 (new visual world). Mode: Persuade. Same copy as the homepage, word for word (all content from `src/lib/site.ts` and the homepage components). Homepage untouched.

Audience: Bengaluru families, mostly women and parents, on phones. Job: decide to contact the clinic. Action: Book a consultation (WhatsApp form), or call. Proof: doctor's credentials, 10+ years, focus areas, medicine sourcing. No reviews or invented numbers. Constraint from the user: must never feel cold or clinical.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the clinic's OP card, the patient card every Indian family keeps for its doctor; it refuses the centred-headline-over-icon-cards clinic template.
OWN-WORLD: Card stocks carry colour at page scale: leaf green (main card), pink (women's health), pale yellow (children), on a soft sage card-file ground. Printed in deep forest-green ink; violet rubber-stamp ink for stamps and chosen state; clay only for attention. Fine keyline rules, one weight everywhere; punched hole, small radius, boxed fields with tiny labels. One sturdy printed serif for titles (Erode), Hanken Grotesk for body.
STORY: The visitor reads the card: who the doctor is (stamped credentials), what she treats (department cards), how visits run (review-date rows), then fills in the card to book.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav as the card file's header strip.
